Contact & Support

Need help with a Skill Link Worldwide account, worker listing, job request, message, booking or review?

Email support

support@skilllinkworldwide.com

What to include

Tell us what happened, the feature involved and any relevant booking or listing details. Do not email passwords, complete payment-card numbers, government identification numbers or other sensitive information.

Service status

Skill Link Worldwide is an early-stage marketplace beginning with the Charlotte, North Carolina area. Some features are still in testing. Online payments, escrow-style fund handling and credential verification are not yet available.

Safety

For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For concerns about a worker, customer, misleading listing or suspicious message, contact support.
