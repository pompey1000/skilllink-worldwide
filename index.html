Booking requests · Test mode

Request and manage bookings

Choose Request Booking on a worker listing, or select a conversation in Messages. Customers can request a booking; workers can accept or decline. No payments are processed here. After a worker accepts, they can mark the work finished and the customer can confirm completion and review. Planned payment policy: customers will have 3 days (72 hours) after the worker marks the job finished to confirm completion or report a problem. Unconfirmed jobs will require administrative review; payouts will not be automatically released. Payments and this review workflow are not yet active and depend on payment-provider approval.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does Skill Link charge a monthly fee?

The planned launch model has no monthly provider subscription. A percentage-based platform fee is planned for completed jobs; the exact fee is not yet set.

Can I pay through the website?

Not yet. Payments, tips and fund-release workflows will be added only after a suitable provider is integrated and the terms are confirmed.

Are professionals verified?

Verification is planned, but it is not active in this demo. Customers should check licensing, insurance and qualifications before hiring.

Where will Skill Link launch?

Charlotte, North Carolina is the planned starting market, with broader expansion later.
